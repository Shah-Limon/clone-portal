Glossary

Common forex bonus & promotion terms, explained.

D

Deposit Bonus

A bonus awarded when you fund a live trading account. Credited as a percentage of your deposit amount.

N

No Deposit Bonus

A free bonus awarded to new clients upon registration, allowing live trading without risking your own funds.

D

Demo Contest

A trading competition held on demo accounts where participants can win cash prizes without risking real money.

L

Live Contest

Contest held on Live account — deposit required — with bigger cash prizes.

F

Forex Gift

A gift for clients/partners for completing certain requirements — from a bonus to the latest hi-tech gadget.

R

Rebate

Cashback for withdrawable bonus per traded lot.

A

Affiliate / IB

Receive a commission from your fellow traders. Specially designed for marketers.

F

Free VPS

Access to an optimized Forex Virtual Private Server for free when maintaining a minimum balance.
