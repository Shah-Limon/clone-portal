    Glossary

    Common forex bonus & promotion terms, explained.

    D

    Deposit Bonus

    A bonus awarded when you fund a live trading account. It is credited as a percentage of your deposit amount.

    N

    No Deposit Bonus

    A free bonus awarded to new clients upon account registration, allowing them to trade on a live account without risking their own funds.

    D

    Demo Contest

    A trading competition held on demo accounts, where participants can win cash prizes or tradable funds without risking their own money.

    L

    Live contest

    Contest held on Live account, Deposit required. Win bigger Cash/Prizes.

    F

    Forex Gift

    A Gift for the clients/Partners for completing certain requirements, everything from a bonus to the latest hi-tech gadget.

    R

    Rebate

    Cashback for Withdrawable Bonus per traded lot.

    A

    Affiliate/IB

    Receive a commission from your fellow traders, specially designed for the marketers.

    F

    Free VPS

    Get access to an optimized Forex Virtual Private Server for free on maintaining a certain amount of trading balance.
